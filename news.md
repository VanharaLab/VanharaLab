<b>June 2026: We received funding from the [IOBC Foundation](https://iocbfoundation.cz/en/projects/117/voucher-program-to-bridge-funding-gaps-in-clinical-research) Voucher Program to bridge funding gaps in clinical research.</b>   

We will investigate, whether biomechanical profiling of PDAC can provide clinically relevant information, such as risk of early relapse or disease progression. Here we will closely cooperate with colleagues from [Nanobiotechnology Core Facility of CEITEC MU](https://www.ceitec.eu/nanobiotechnology-core-facility/cf104) and Surgery Clinic and Department of Pathology of the [University Hospital Brno](https://www.fnbrno.cz/en/the-university-hospital-brno/t1000).
